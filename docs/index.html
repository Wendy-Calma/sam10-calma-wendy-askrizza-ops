What it is

A helpdesk that knows its campus, and knows its limits

AskRizza answers practical questions from students of Rizza Polytechnic, using a short collection of campus facts that I wrote. When an answer isn't in those facts, it says so and points the student to the Facilities Office. It never makes decisions about individual people.

I didn't build a chatbot platform from scratch. AskRizza is LibreChat (MIT licence), reskinned with a new name, logo, colours, wording and campus content. The answers come from Google Gemini, which runs outside AWS. My work was the part around it: deploying it to AWS and running it properly.
