# Rizza Polytechnic - Campus Facilities Facts (FICTIONAL)
All names, places, numbers and people below are invented for a student project. Rizza Polytechnic does not exist.

## Offices
- Facilities Office: Room A-104, Admin Building (Building A). Mon-Fri 08:00-17:00. Handles repairs, room bookings, aircon problems, lost & found, lockers. Email: facilities@rizza.example
- IT Service Desk: Room L-012, Library basement (Building L). Mon-Fri 08:00-19:00, Sat 09:00-12:00. Handles Wi-Fi, campus accounts, printing, lab computers. Email: itdesk@rizza.example
- Security Post: Main Gate (Gate 1). Open 24 hours, every day. Handles ID access problems after hours, parking stickers, emergencies on campus.
- Registrar: Room A-201. Mon-Fri 09:00-16:00. Handles enrollment documents and certificates. The helpdesk cannot answer questions about grades, admissions or individual records.

## Building hours
- Library (Building L): Mon-Fri 07:30-21:00, Sat 08:00-17:00, closed Sunday and public holidays. During final exam week (the last two weeks of each term) it is open until 23:00 Mon-Sat.
- Engineering Labs (Building E): Mon-Fri 08:00-20:00. Weekend access only with a lab-access request approved by the department, submitted at least 2 working days before.
- Gym and Sports Hall (Building G): Mon-Sat 06:00-20:00. Bring a valid school ID. Towels are not provided.
- Canteen (Building C): Mon-Fri 07:00-19:00, Sat 07:00-14:00.
- Admin Building (Building A): Mon-Fri 08:00-17:00.

## Room bookings
- Classrooms and the three meeting rooms (A-110, A-111, L-220) are booked through the online RoomFinder form on the student portal.
- Book at least 1 working day in advance. Maximum booking length is 3 hours. Maximum 2 active bookings per student organisation.
- The Auditorium (Building A, ground floor) needs a written request to the Facilities Office at least 10 working days in advance.
- Cancel unused bookings in RoomFinder; three no-shows in a term suspends booking rights for that organisation for the rest of the term.

## Wi-Fi and IT
- Student Wi-Fi network: RP-Student. Log in with your campus account (same as the student portal).
- Guest Wi-Fi: RP-Guest. Guests get a 1-day code from the IT Service Desk or the Library front desk.
- Forgotten password: reset it at the student portal "Forgot password" link. If locked out after 5 failed attempts, wait 30 minutes or visit the IT Service Desk with your school ID.
- Printing: Library ground floor and Building E lobby. Black-and-white costs 2 credits per page, colour 10 credits per page. Credits are topped up at the Library front desk.

## Parking
- Student parking is in Lot B (behind the Gym). A parking sticker is required; get it at the Security Post with your vehicle registration and school ID. Stickers are valid for one academic year.
- Visitor parking is in Lot A (near Gate 1), maximum 4 hours.
- Motorcycles park in the covered area beside Lot B. Bicycles use the racks outside the Library and the Gym, free, no sticker needed.

## Lost and found
- Items found on campus are brought to the Facilities Office (A-104). After office hours, hand them to the Security Post at Gate 1.
- Items are kept for 30 days, then donated. ID cards and wallets are kept for 60 days.
- To claim an item, describe it and show your school ID.

## Lockers
- Lockers are in Building E (ground floor) and the Library basement. Rent per term at the Facilities Office. Lockers must be emptied by the last day of each term; contents left behind go to lost and found.

## Repairs and problems
- Report broken facilities (aircon, lights, leaks, broken chairs, projectors) at the Facilities Office or by email to facilities@rizza.example. Include building, room number and a short description.
- Urgent safety problems (flooding, electrical sparks, gas smell) - tell the Security Post at Gate 1 immediately; it is open 24 hours.
- Typical repair time: urgent same day; normal requests within 3 working days.

## Campus calendar
- The campus is closed on public holidays. The Security Post stays open.
- Term breaks: Library opens Mon-Fri 08:00-17:00 only.
